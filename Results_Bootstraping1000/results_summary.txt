Interpretation:
Completion = readable/completed audits divided by all attempted audits.
End-to-end recall = detected seeded errors divided by all seeded opportunities.
Conditional recall = detected seeded errors divided by opportunities in completed audits.
Conditional recall uses different successful subsets for different LLMs; it is not a fair standalone ranking.
Six base workbooks are only six clusters. Nominal 95% intervals have uncertain coverage.
The workbooks are a selected case study, not a random sample of all real-estate models.
Zero failures in GPT produce a degenerate completion interval; this does not prove universal readability.
The bootstrap retains the observed repeats. It does not separately model future-run randomness.
No p-values, guaranteed significance, or guaranteed +/-10% margin are claimed.
Precision, F1 and conventional accuracy are omitted: exhaustive findings and a defined TN population are unavailable.
Original workbooks are unchanged. Corrected audit counts are supplied as CSV.
Reference: Cameron & Miller (2015), A Practitioner's Guide to Cluster-Robust Inference.
